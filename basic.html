How To Use AI Class

AI Class contains 20 Basic 9 subjects. Each subject contains exactly 13 topics, giving you 13 topics per term, with separate First, Second and Third Term lessons.

1. Choose a Subject

Open Home and choose the subject you want to study.

2. Choose a Topic

Each subject has 13 topics. Select any topic to begin.

3. Read the Lesson

Every lesson contains an introduction, meaning, detailed explanation, steps, examples, key points, common mistakes and an exercise.

4. Complete the Exercise

You must complete the exercise correctly before the Next button becomes available.

5. Track Progress

Your completed lessons are stored in your browser using local storage.

6. Revise

The Revision section allows you to select the exact subject and topic you want to review.

7. Ask the AI Teacher

Choose a subject and topic and ask a question. The offline AI Teacher uses the lesson information stored inside this website.

8. Take Mock Exams

Select a subject and take a 13-question examination. Questions are based on the topics in that subject.

9.Use settings

open the setting button to change user Name, theme Colour, acivate dark mode and resetProgress.
